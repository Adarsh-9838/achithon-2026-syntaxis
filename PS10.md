## Domain

Open Innovation / Hardware / Embedded Systems / IoT



## Problem Statement

Develop an innovative hardware-based solution that addresses a meaningful real-world challenge in areas such as healthcare, agriculture, environmental monitoring, public safety, energy management, accessibility, or smart infrastructure.

The proposed system should combine suitable electronic components, sensors, microcontrollers, actuators, or communication modules to perform a useful task. It should demonstrate a functional hardware prototype, explain how the solution improves on existing approaches, and provide measurable evidence of its effectiveness through testing.

Unlike a narrowly defined problem statement, this category gives participants the freedom to identify a problem independently and design a solution around it.

### What the Problem Statement Expects

The objective is not simply to assemble a sensor and display its readings. The project should demonstrate a complete engineering solution:

- Real-world problem: Identify a specific issue experienced by a target group or community.
- Hardware innovation: Use physical components to detect, measure, control, automate, or prevent something.
- Functional prototype: Build a working system rather than presenting only a concept or simulation.
- Practical usefulness: Show how the solution improves cost, accessibility, reliability, safety, efficiency, or convenience.
- Measurable evaluation: Test the prototype under realistic conditions and report results.
- Feasibility: Keep the design affordable, reproducible, and achievable within the available development time.

## Key Deliverable

A complete submission should ideally contain:

- A clearly defined real-world problem and intended users.
- A functional hardware prototype with documented components.
- A block diagram and circuit schematic.
- Embedded software implementing the system logic.
- A demonstration of the principal functionality.
- Experimental results covering accuracy, response time, reliability, or energy consumption as appropriate.
- A cost breakdown and explanation of how the solution could be deployed.
- A report identifying limitations, safety considerations, and future improvements.
