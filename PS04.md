
## Domain

HealthTech / Generative AI / Edge AI


## Problem Statement

Healthcare professionals in rural and underserved regions frequently work in environments with limited or unreliable internet connectivity. Patient information is often distributed across handwritten notes, paper-based medical reports, verbal consultations, and fragmented digital records. Manually converting these sources into structured medical documentation is time-consuming and can lead to incomplete records, transcription errors, and difficulties accessing relevant patient history.

The proposed project aims to develop an offline-first, multimodal AI assistant that enables healthcare workers to capture, organize, and retrieve patient information even when internet access is unavailable. The application will support voice-based clinical note capture, speech transcription, language translation where supported, medical document scanning, optical character recognition (OCR), and AI-assisted conversion of unstructured information into structured patient case files.

A lightweight, on-device AI pipeline will process supported inputs locally, while a local database will maintain patient records, consultation histories, and pending changes. Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) will help organize relevant historical information and generate structured clinical summaries for healthcare professionals to review.

When connectivity is restored, a secure synchronization engine will transfer pending updates to a centralized database. It will use unique operation identifiers, version tracking, retry-safe requests, and explicit conflict detection and resolution to prevent duplicate updates and avoid silently discarding concurrent changes.

The system will prioritize data integrity, patient privacy, usability on resource-constrained devices, and continued operation during network interruptions. AI-generated information will remain editable and subject to clinician verification.

## Key Deliverable

A functional offline-to-online healthcare documentation system that converts supported voice notes and medical document images into structured, clinician-reviewable case files; maintains searchable local patient histories; and synchronizes encrypted changes to a central database when connectivity returns.

The primary engineering deliverable will be a reliable synchronization engine that preserves offline changes, safely retries interrupted operations, detects concurrent modifications, and provides a verifiable conflict-resolution workflow.

### Expected Outcome

The project will demonstrate how edge AI, multimodal information extraction, local data persistence, and reliable synchronization can be combined to support clinical documentation in low-connectivity healthcare environments. Its success will be evaluated through offline usability, extraction accuracy, synchronization reliability, conflict-handling tests, data-integrity checks, and performance on resource-constrained devices.
