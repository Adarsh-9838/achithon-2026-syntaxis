## Domain

Constraint Optimization and Decision Support


## Problem Statement

Organizations allocating limited seats across training programs must balance applicant eligibility, program capacity, individual preferences, and previously confirmed assignments. When applicants withdraw or program capacities change, existing allocations may become invalid, requiring reassignment and potentially creating dissatisfaction or disputes.

Manual allocation methods and simple reassignment rules may produce inconsistent results, unnecessarily disrupt existing placements, or fail to explain why particular applicants received or lost their preferred options. Furthermore, evaluating individual appeals can be difficult because accommodating one applicant may affect other applicants, available capacity, and the overall quality of the allocation.

The proposed project aims to develop FAIRSHIFT, an auditable allocation and decision-support system that uses mathematical constraint optimization to produce valid assignments while minimizing unnecessary disruption.

The system will represent applicants, programs, eligibility requirements, seat capacities, ranked preferences, and existing assignments as a formal optimization model. Using Python and Google OR-Tools CP-SAT, it will generate an initial allocation that satisfies mandatory constraints while optimizing preference satisfaction and preserving valid existing assignments wherever possible.

When applicants withdraw or program capacities change, the engine will recompute the allocation and identify which assignments must change and which can remain unchanged. A counterfactual appeal module will evaluate requested placements by comparing the baseline allocation with an alternative solution that accommodates the request, where feasible. The resulting analysis will quantify effects on other applicants, reassignment counts, preference satisfaction, and constraint compliance.

An independent validation component will verify the resulting assignments against the declared rules. The system will also distinguish proven infeasibility from unresolved solver outcomes, including timeouts, and will provide transparent explanations of assignment changes and optimization trade-offs.

The final deliverable will be a functional web application capable of managing approximately 200 applicants and 10 programs, demonstrating initial allocation, capacity-change reassignment, independent constraint validation, and counterfactual appeal analysis.

## Key Deliverable

A functional web application that supports initial applicant-to-program allocation, reassignment after capacity changes or withdrawals, independent constraint validation, and counterfactual appeal analysis.

The application will display assignment quality, preference satisfaction, reassignment counts, affected applicants, and solver status. It will distinguish optimal solutions from feasible but unproven solutions, proven infeasibility, and unresolved outcomes such as timeouts.

The system will also maintain an audit trail of allocation changes and provide evidence-based explanations showing how accommodating an individual request affects other assignments and the overall allocation.

### Expected Outcome

FAIRSHIFT will demonstrate how constraint programming and decision-support techniques can produce valid, transparent, and minimally disruptive allocations under changing conditions.

Its effectiveness will be evaluated through constraint compliance, preference satisfaction, reassignment counts, counterfactual accuracy, solver performance, audit completeness, and the correctness of reported solver statuses.
